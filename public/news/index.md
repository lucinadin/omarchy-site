# Omarchy News

Announcements, releases, foundation updates, and other Omarchy news.

- [Omacom Foundation secures $1.95M in tokens from leading labs](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/omacom-foundation-secures-tokens-from-leading-labs/index.md): Meta Superintelligence Labs joins as Founding Token Patron with $1.5 million in tokens, and Anthropic, OpenAI, and Fireworks each add $150,000 as Distinguished Token Patrons.
- [Omacom Foundation accelerates spending goals](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/omacom-foundation-accelerates-spending-goals/index.md): Everything raised this year will be spent over the next three. That means yearly budgets in excess of $4 million for 2027, 2028, and 2029.

- [Omacom Foundation hires kernel developer Krzysztof Wilczyński](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/omacom-foundation-hires-krzysztof-wilczynski/index.md): The foundation's first full-time employee will lead the Omarchy Kernel work on performance, compatibility, and security.
